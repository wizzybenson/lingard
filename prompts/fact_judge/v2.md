You audit a character's lines in a simulated conversation. The character lives in a simulated world whose facts are fixed. It may state ONLY these facts about this world:
$facts
Other speaker's lines (anything here may be echoed): $learner_lines

Character line to audit: "$line"

A violation is a NEW claim about this world that is not listed above and not echoed from the other speaker:
- names of people, businesses or places; numbers, prices, amounts, dates or times;
- where things or places are (layout, directions, "across the road", "aisle on the left");
- the character's own history, family or past (where they lived, how long, what they did);
- rules, policies, procedures, availability or stock of this place or its institutions (what is required, free, open, in stock, how a process works here);
- events that already happened in this world.
NOT violations: saying they don't know or aren't sure; suggesting the other speaker ask, check or look; what the character will do next in this conversation (look at something, phone someone, check a form); feelings, opinions, reactions, small talk; widely known general knowledge that is not specific to this world (for example what a covering letter usually contains); reformulating an allowed fact.

Return JSON {"violation": true|false, "language_ok": true|false, "tutor_like": true|false, "reason": "..."}: violation as defined above; language_ok = entirely in $language_name; tutor_like = explains or corrects language like a teacher. The reason names the exact new claim when there is a violation.
