## Write
- learner_goal: one short sentence in $language_name, the visible goal (e.g. "Get your deposit back").
- elicitation: for EVERY target above except pronunciation targets, one tactic: a concrete thing the character does or asks that makes using the target natural. Written in English for the actor.
- approved_facts: 3–6 short concrete facts (in $language_name) the character may state in this scene: amounts, dates, objects, events. Everything the scene needs must be here; the character can say nothing else factual.
- success_conditions: 1–3 observable things the learner must do to achieve the goal.
- evidence_required: for the 1–2 most important grammar or function targets, min_uses (usually 1).
- exits: 1–3 graceful in-world endings with consequences (agreement reached, learner walks out, etc.).
- max_turns: between 10 and 30.
- mood: the character's mood at the start.

Use only the target keys listed above. Keep it realistic, respectful and free of stereotypes.
