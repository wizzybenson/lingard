# 0022 — Curriculum scope audit: rules, words, functions, sounds and every exam task variant

Date: 2026-10-20 · Status: accepted

## Context

The founder asked for the curriculum to cover the full range of both languages, from language rules to every axis of
both exams.

  The letter "may be personal, semi-formal or formal". The essay asks for an opinion, a discussion of ideas, or a

## What changed

| Axis | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| French grammar | 32 points | 51: c'est/il est/il y a, adjective agreement, possessives and demonstratives, prepositions with places, quantities, reflexive verbs, venir de/en train de, passé composé vs imparfait, depuis/il y a/pendant/dans, adverbs, indefinites and ne…que, double pronouns, celui/le mien, futur antérieur, past-participle agreement, subjunctive after conjunctions, infinitif passé, lequel/auquel, nuanced concession |
| English grammar | 27 | 48: there is/it is, subject–verb agreement, frequency adverbs, pronouns and possessives, in/on/at, can/could/be able to, quantifiers, zero conditional and time clauses, present perfect vs past simple, too/enough/so/such, phrasal verbs, substitution, present perfect continuous, deduction, unless/in case, word formation, past modals, causative, future continuous/perfect, hedging, nominalisation |
| Vocabulary sets | 17 per language | 32: numbers/dates/times, directions, family, home, bills, sport, travel, immigration and citizenship, work rights, money, culture, law, media, society, science and public health |
| Functions | 21 | 33: directions, invitations, thanks, the phone, suggestions, permission, sympathy, certainty, instructions, register, reporting, structuring a talk |
| Pronunciation | 12–13 targets | 15–16: /s/–/z/, /i/–/y/, /ʃ/–/ʒ/ in French; /w/–/v/, /æ/–/ʌ/, final -s in English |
| IELTS writing | 3 prompts per task, 3 essay types | Variants declared per task: personal, semi-formal and formal letters; opinion, discussion, advantages/disadvantages, problem–solution and two-part essays. At least 2 prompts per variant (16 prompts) |
| TEF writing | 3 prompts per section | 6 per section; section B as letter to the editor and forum post |
| IELTS speaking | 3 per part; Part 3 drawn at random | 5 Part 1, 6 Part 2 and 6 Part 3 tasks. A mock's Part 3 discusses the theme of its Part 2 cue card (`topic`) |

Tests now hold this scope in place:
- the audited grammar list for each language;
- grammar at every band from 4 to 10;
- prerequisites at the same band or lower;
- at least two examples and one common error per lesson;
- the same vocabulary and function sets in both languages;
- at least two prompts per writing variant;
- Part 3 linked to Part 2.

## Still open

- Vocabulary sets list words but no definitions or example sentences. They are practised in scenes, quests and
  remedial lessons, but are not taught as "learn first" lessons the way grammar is.
- TEF speaking has 3 tasks per section. That is enough for mocks, but practice would benefit from more.
- The new content is original and checked by its author (an AI). A human teacher's review is recommended before wide
  use, as SPEC §17 asks for all content.
